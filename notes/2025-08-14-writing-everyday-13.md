Ever wonder if we have free will or if we are just a product of genetics, environment, or even just our **birth order**.

A while back I was listening to a podcast with a behavioral scientist Gad Saad.
Saad mentioned an	idea that younger siblings take more risks and are more rebellious, and he shared some psychological theories on why that may be from the book "Born to Rebel" by Frank Sulloway (now on my reading list).

During this discussion a study survey idea was raised:
Correlation between birth order of individuals and their partners.
The team actually created a survey and posted results.
If like me you are curious, feel free to view the results [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1f3YZdPaAg7t3TknwKfuY0OUmb0wt-b2g3_0Ct7HBzVE/edit?tab=t.0).

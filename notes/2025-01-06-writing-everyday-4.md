Today in a conversation I was asked what i would do if I had 5 million dollars. This question stopped me for a moment, and i decided I needed time to think on it.
Here are ~~ten~~ eight things I came up with. The order does not correlate with significance.
1. Self fund companies, and find people to run them; focusing on projects I believe in to benefit people as opposed to the startup mindset to 30x.
2. Spend time to find a personal assistant, who takes care of all the day to day busywork.
3. Regularly have lunch/dinner plans with people. I believe I am happier when socializing and eating is a big part of the socializing world.
4. Outsource a private tech stack; this includes documents, photos, etc, in secure locations under my control.
5. Start investing more aggressively and deepening my understanding in market strategies.
6. Pay for a close friends education. This was a promise to a specific person, don't get any ideas.
7. Internalize that I have financial safety, and will be able to raise my family with values I hold strongly.
8. Hire acoustic engineers to plan out a room I find calming.

I gave myself the writing prompt: Write a poem based on the image above.
This is what I came up with:

<div style="font-family: cursive;">
Lone ring golden shine,<br />
Lone ring no longer mine<br /><br />
While worn shouting unity,<br />
alone whispering a barren truth<br>
On a stone so cold,<br>
ringing cries of loneliness<br><br>
So beautiful when bare,<br>
depicting a picture of despair<br>
G-d in heaven bring all gold bands,<br>
the heat of a warm hand
</div>

ורווקים ורווקות יהיה להם זיווג
</div>

Have you ever been through a bad breakup?
I don't have an issue with the person I ~~broke up with~~ divorced, but past friends/family is another story.

I think a few years of being told one thing and then hearing another opened a wound.
The type of wound that stings.
The scab itches unbearably I can't refrain from reopening it.

I frequently find myself obsessing and rewriting a message looking for something I missed.
Is this something I will be called horrible things for?
With my imagination (maybe rumination) anything can be!

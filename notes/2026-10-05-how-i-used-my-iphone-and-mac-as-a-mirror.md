Did you know, you can use your iphone and mac as a camera/monitor duo?
But, why would you want to?

I once tried cutting my own hair using two phones on a video call.
The latency caused me to misjudge my hand position and take out a nice strip of hair on the back of my head.

Connecting the iphone to the mac gave me a better live feed and a bigger screen watch how I mangle my hair.


If you are looking for high resolution views of the back of your head, check out the settings below:

- Settings on iphone:<br>
General -> Airplay & Continuity -> Continuity Camera
>	Or just search continuity camera
- On Mac:<br>
Quicktime -> file -> new movie -> arrow near record button -> iphone as camera

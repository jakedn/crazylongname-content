I am writing a book about my experience with my ex-wife of 6 years, with excerpts from her point of view.

## Why write a book?
Recently, I have had a newfound appreciation for sharing ideas and experiences with other people.
I am starting to internalize the importance of collective wisdom in the journey that is our lives.  

## What makes this book different?
This book will tell the story of two different people;<br>
their decisions to get married during a trying time in life;<br>
how two well meaning people brought out the worst in each other;<br>
how through **love and mutual support** they **ended** their marriage.
